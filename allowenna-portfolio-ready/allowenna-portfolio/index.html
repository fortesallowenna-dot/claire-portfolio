A little about me

About Me

A student designer learning to build software that people genuinely enjoy using.

I'm a Computer Science student working toward a career as a UI/UX designer. I love the space where design meets technology: interfaces that look good and, more importantly, feel simple and natural to use.

Right now I'm learning to sketch layouts, create wireframes and prototypes, and map out how someone moves through a product, step by step. My programming background helps me see how a design becomes working software.

This portfolio collects my skills and certificates, from HTML, JavaScript, and C coursework to my on-the-job training at SPDRRMO. I'm always open to feedback, fresh ideas, and new opportunities.
